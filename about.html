About the Portal

Improving complaint handling through a centralized digital platform.

Online Complaint Management Portal

The Online Complaint Management Portal is a web-based system designed to simplify complaint registration, assignment, tracking and resolution.

Users can submit complaints through an online account and monitor their progress without repeatedly visiting an office. Administrators can organize complaints by category and department, while staff members can work on complaints assigned to them.

The portal maintains complaint status history so users can understand how their complaint moves through different stages of the resolution process.

Main Objectives

Online Registration

Allow users to register complaints through a convenient web interface.

Transparency

Give users visibility into the current status and history of their complaints.

Organized Workflow

Route complaints to suitable departments and responsible staff members.

Better Monitoring

Provide administrators with dashboards and reports for complaint monitoring.

System Roles

User

Registers, logs in, submits complaints, checks status, updates profile and submits feedback.

Staff

Views assigned complaints, checks complaint details and updates complaint progress.

Administrator

Controls complaints, users, staff, categories, departments, reports and contact messages.
