What You Can Do

A complete complaint lifecycle from submission to resolution.

Submit Complaints

Register complaints with category, priority, location, description and supporting image.

Track Status

Monitor complaint progress from pending and assigned through in-progress and resolution.

Department Management

Complaints can be routed to appropriate departments and assigned to responsible staff members.

Feedback

Citizens can provide ratings and feedback about the complaint resolution process.

Complaint Resolution Process

Four simple steps help manage each complaint.

1

Create Account

Register using your basic information and login to your personal dashboard.

2

Submit Complaint

Select a category and provide detailed information about the issue.

3

Staff Processing

The administrator assigns the complaint to the appropriate department and staff member.

4

Track & Resolve

Follow status updates until the complaint is resolved or closed.
