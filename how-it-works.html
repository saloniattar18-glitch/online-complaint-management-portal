How It Works

Understand the complete complaint management workflow.

1

Register

Create a user account using your name, email, mobile number and password.

2

Login

Login using your registered email and password to access your personal dashboard.

3

Submit Complaint

Provide a complaint title, category, priority, description, location and optional image.

4

Admin Review

The administrator reviews the complaint and assigns it to an appropriate department and staff member.

5

Staff Action

Assigned staff members review complaints and update their progress with remarks.

6

Resolution

Once the issue is handled, the complaint can be marked as resolved or closed.

7

Track History

Users can check the complaint's status timeline and review each update.

8

Feedback

Users can provide ratings and comments based on their experience.

Complaint Status Flow

Pending Assigned In Progress Resolved Closed

Depending on the situation, an administrator may also reject an invalid or inappropriate complaint.
